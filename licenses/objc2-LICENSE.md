# objc2 Rust crate licenses

Applies to objc2-core-foundation and objc2-io-kit 0.3.2, source commit
7b1abfd750a2cacaea71d6a56ecfb83cb7de560b in the upstream madsmtm/objc2
repository. Their Cargo metadata declares Zlib OR Apache-2.0 OR MIT.

The upstream license notice states that these crates and the objc2, block2,
objc2-foundation, and objc2-encode crates have distinct licensing terms;
other crates in that repository are offered under Zlib, Apache-2.0, or MIT at
the recipient's option. These bindings are derived from Apple SDKs. The upstream
notice points to Apple's Xcode license and notes that its distribution terms
for derived works are unclear. The license files for all three SPDX alternatives
are included in the Rust dependency bundle when available from the locked crate
sources.

Upstream source: LICENSE.md at the commit above. SPDX license texts are
provided by the matching license files included in THIRD_PARTY_RUST_LICENSES.md.
